## Data that does not exist
- **Rome metro**: there is no public figure for violence or thefts on the metro. There are only news reports of arrests by the metro police and an unsourced "+35% pickpocketing", so it is not in the video.
- **Official bulletins of London and Paris**: the Met Police PDFs and the interieur.gouv.fr pages refuse automated requests. That is why the numbers come from London City Hall and from the SSMSI database on data.gouv.fr.
